Why install Novvy?

A tiny app that turns your phone into a multi-agent AI workspace — no bloat, no lock-in.

🧠

Multi-agent by design

An orchestrator breaks your goal into sub-tasks and worker agents solve them with tools — then it all comes back as one answer.

🔌

Bring your own model

Works with Claude, OpenAI, a local Ollama, or the built-in offline demo. Switch providers anytime — you're never locked in.

🛠️

Agents that use tools

Real reasoning loops call tools (calculator, text stats, and more you can add) instead of just chatting.

🔒

Private & self-hostable

Run the backend on your own machine or server. Your data and prompts stay yours — nothing is forced through a third party.

🪶

Lightweight & fast

A ~780 KB app with a clean, no-nonsense UI. No ads, no accounts, no subscriptions.

🌍

Open source

MIT-licensed and fully on GitHub. Inspect it, fork it, extend it, or self-host it.

⭐ Star on GitHub MIT licensed No ads · No tracking

Install over USB (no terminal)

Connect your phone via USB with USB debugging enabled, then check for devices and pick one. Works in desktop Chrome or Edge over HTTPS.
